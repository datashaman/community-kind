# Governance

CommunityKind begins with the repository owner as lead maintainer, accountable
for fair review, roadmap clarity, releases, and security response. Maintainer
rights are earned through sustained, reviewed contribution and may be revoked
for inactivity, abuse, conflicts of interest, or failure to uphold the project's
licensing, safety, accessibility, and privacy commitments.

Significant product and architecture proposals are discussed publicly and
recorded in the PRD or architecture decisions. Exceptions are limited to
time-bounded security embargoes and sanitized adopter-confidential decisions.

Contributors retain copyright under the DCO process. Governance cannot silently
relicense their work into a proprietary edition.
